# Co-marketing action types

| Action | Our side | Partner side | Fits when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Partner page and announcement | Catalog entry and article, per the partner launch runbook (the CTO runs go-live) | Approves page, logos, claims | The agreement is filed and the integration or programme is live |
| Joint social post | Same day and hour, our own voice | Posts with their tracking link | Both have an active audience on the same network |
| Newsletter mention | Short copy, one link with UTM | Includes it in their edition | The partner owns a newsletter or community |
| Joint template | A validated template for their clients | Shares it with their users | The partner has a clear client use case |
| Listing | A marketplace or directory listing (partner-marketplace-listings) | A listing on their side | Either side runs a marketplace or directory |
| Webinar or live demo | Demo of one product on their use case | Invites their audience | The partner has an audience that buys tools live |

## Relationship status words

- "is live": working now, verified.
- "is signing" or "signed": agreement in progress or filed.
- "both teams are building": jointly planned, in writing.
- "the proposed programme", "subject to joint approval": proposed only.

## Copy rules

- Lead with the customer outcome, never "we are excited".
- Show why the pair is stronger than either product alone.
- Name the motion: co-build, co-sell, partner-sourced, integrator enablement, reseller, referral, pilot.
- No hype words, no em or en dashes, no emoji in page or article copy.
- Program levels and certifications exactly as granted by the third party.
