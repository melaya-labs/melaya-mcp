# Partner onboarding kit contents

Everything below comes from the knowledge base. Leave out a section rather than invent it.

## 1. Programme one-pager

- Their tier and what it pays, exactly as in the programme terms note.
- Attribution window, approval period, payout cadence and threshold, exclusions.
- How to open their dashboard and find their tracking link.

## 2. What to sell

- The one or two products that fit their clients, each with three concrete workflows.
- Links to the public docs and validated templates for those products.
- The first-success path a client follows (from the CXO activation paths).

## 3. Assets

- Brand kit: logos, screenshots, approved descriptions, partner badge if one exists.
- Programme creatives from the affiliate platform (`affonso_list_creatives` if Affonso).

## 4. Wording rules

- Claims they may make and claims they must never make, from the claims policy.
- Programme or certification labels exactly as granted by any third party; never upgrade them.
- No client pilots or NDA partners named.

## 5. Support route

- Where partners ask partner questions (the partner mailbox from connector settings).
- Where their clients get help (the public help path).
- When and how to bring the team into a client call.

## 6. For integrators

- The rollout journey their client follows (install, connect, model or provider, templates, approvals, handover), from the integrator materials.
- How to book an enablement call.

## Checks before sending

- Every link opens today.
- Only shipped products and integrations are described.
- Tier terms match the live programme read this turn.
