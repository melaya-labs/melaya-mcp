# Matching a partner to a tier

The tiers, rates and benefits come from the programme terms note. This file only says how to choose among them.

## Ask first

- Who are your clients or audience, and how many?
- Do you implement and keep the client, refer and step away, or resell?
- Does your audience respond to a launch discount, or to expertise?
- Which one or two of our products fit your clients?

## Typical mapping

| Partner behaviour | Usually fits | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Implements and keeps the client relationship (integrator, agency) | The tier with the highest recurring commission and no audience discount | Their clients stay long; the partner carries the work |
| Community, newsletter or course with an engaged audience | A tier that trades some commission for a modest audience discount | A small perk lifts conversion without training the audience to wait for deals |
| Launch community or deal-seeking audience | A tier that trades more commission for a stronger, time-limited audience discount | A strong first offer drives the first purchase |
| Reseller who invoices the client | Not a standing tier: owner decision | Invoice ownership, margins and support boundaries need an agreement |

## Rules

- One tier per partner, with the reason written in the offer note.
- Never combine a tier's audience discount with another discount.
- Follow the recorded switch rules (cooldown, lock after taking an audience discount); they stop a partner from handing out discounts and then collecting the higher rate on the same customers.
- Integrators and resellers join the same programme unless the terms note says otherwise.
- Excluded referrals (self-referrals, own company accounts, existing customers) are stated in every offer.
