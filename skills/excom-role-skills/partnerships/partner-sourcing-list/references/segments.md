# Partner segments and scoring

## Default segments

| Segment | Who | Why they partner |
|---|---|---|
| Integrators | Implementers of the CRMs, ERPs and collaboration tools your product connects to | They add an AI practice and keep the client |
| Agencies | Marketing, SEO, growth and RevOps agencies | They deliver outcomes for clients with your product |
| Consultancies | AI and automation consultants | They need a governed platform to deliver on |
| Marketplaces and registries | App marketplaces where your product installs, tool and agent registries | Distribution and discovery |
| Technology partners | Products with an API or MCP server your product can call | Joint workflows, connector listing, co-marketing |
| Launch communities | Product launch sites, builder communities, newsletters | Reach to early adopters |

Adjust to the partner archetypes in the knowledge base when they exist.

## Scoring (0 to 100)

| Criterion | Points | High score looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Client overlap with one product | 40 | Their clients need a job one product does well |
| Ability to sell or implement | 25 | A team that delivers projects or has a sales motion |
| Reach in the segment | 20 | Known in the segment, clients or audience of meaningful size |
| Timely reason to write | 15 | A launch, a new integration, a hiring post, a public client need this month |

## Rules

- Zero names already in any ledger, CRM or the affiliate platform.
- Every contact route is a published business address verified this turn.
- One product fit and one specific reason per name.
- Client pilots and partners under NDA are never listed.
- No personal data beyond business name, role and business email.
