# Partner agreement terms checklist

## Standard, fillable from the knowledge base

- Parties: legal names and signers, as confirmed in writing.
- Programme tier and its commission and audience benefit, exactly as in the programme terms note.
- Attribution window, approval period, payout cadence and threshold, exclusions.
- Brand use and claim rules from the brand and claims notes.

## Open by default: the owner decides each, with a default recommendation

1. Commercial path and invoice ownership.
2. Margins, revenue share beyond the tier, minimums, usage overages, taxes, refunds and collections.
3. Territory, vertical, exclusivity and named accounts.
4. Deal registration, attribution disputes, non-circumvention and survival periods.
5. Support boundaries (first, second and third line), coverage hours, escalation and response targets.
6. Data roles, security evidence, deployment topology, subprocessors and privacy terms.
7. IP ownership, template sharing, SDK terms, branding, attribution and white-label disclosures.
8. Term, termination, transition, data export and continuity support.

## Never put in an agreement unless the claims policy says it is true

- Security certifications (for example SOC 2, ISO 27001).
- A completed independent penetration test.
- A universal SLA, RPO, RTO or service credits.
- Fixed referral percentages, reseller discounts, exclusivity, revenue guarantees or minimum commitments outside the standing tiers.

## Handling partner changes

- Reply with a holding note ("I'll come back to you on this point"), drafted for approval.
- Send the request to the owner with the partner's exact wording and a default.
- Never accept a change in the thread.

## Confidentiality

Signed agreements, terms and signatures stay in DocuSign, the mailbox and the partner's Drive folder. Never copy them into notes or the knowledge base.
