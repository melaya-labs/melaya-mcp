# Partner enablement brief

```text
Partner type: <integrator | agency | community | reseller>
Who buys from them: <client profile>
Products to sell: <one or two, named as in the products note>
Outcome in one sentence: <what the client gets>

Talk track
  Opening line: <one sentence tied to the client's job>
  Discovery questions: volume per month, cloud or own machines, seats, which surface or tool, who approves actions
  Call to action: <free start or a template>

Demo path: <the public first-success tutorial for the product, step by step>

Objections: | objection | approved answer | proof link |

Plan guidance: <fence table from the pricing note, prices with the note's date>

Do not say: <claims never to make from the claims policy; unreleased dates; pilot or NDA names>

System steps: how to use the tracking link, where clients get help, when to bring us into a call
```

## Reinforcement rules

- Touches are short, useful and tied to a real client need.
- At most two touches a month per partner.
- Silent partners get one honest check-in, then a pause.
- Successes are shared (with permission) so other partners see what works.
- Every talk track and demo path is re-checked against the live product when a product changes.
