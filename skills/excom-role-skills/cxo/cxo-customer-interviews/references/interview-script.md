# Interview script and survey rules

## 20-minute interview

1. Context (3 min): "What's your role, and what were you trying to get done when you found us?"
2. Before (3 min): "What did you use or try before? What made you look for something else?"
3. The last real attempt (7 min): "Walk me through the last time you used it, step by step." Ask why up to five times on the key moment.
4. The moment it worked or broke (3 min): "What happened right then? What did you do next?"
5. Value (2 min): "How would you feel if you could no longer use it? Very disappointed, somewhat, not disappointed?"
6. Close (2 min): "Is there anything I should have asked? Who else should I talk to?"

## Interview rules

- Keep it a conversation, not a test; people perform when they feel studied.
- Look for evidence that disproves the hypothesis.
- Ask about past behaviour, not hypothetical futures.
- Never pitch, defend or promise a feature or date.
- Pain points (what hurts) and passion points (what they love) are both data.

## Survey (larger samples)

- One goal, under ten questions, under ten minutes.
- A screener question first; no double-barrelled questions.
- Randomise answer order where it makes sense.
- Include the "very disappointed" question for product-market fit reads.
- Test on two people before sending; check every language version.

## Notes template

```text
Participant: <segment, plan, tenure> (no personal data in shared notes)
Job to be done: <line>
Before: <line>
Key moment: <what happened, their words in quotes>
Why chain: <answer 1 > 2 > 3 ...>
Very disappointed: <yes | somewhat | no>
Referrals: <names only with their consent>
```
