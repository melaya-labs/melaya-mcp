# Macro review checklist

Pass every item before a macro is used. Record pass or fail with a note.

## Accuracy and coverage

- Steps match the live product today; button and screen names exactly as shown in the app.
- The linked doc exists, is public and says the same thing.
- Limits, prices or plan names, if any, match the pricing note and carry its date.
- The macro covers the common variants of the question, or says when it does not apply.

## Voice

- Fix first, reason in one line, link, what to send if it persists.
- Plain text, under 90 words, no markdown.
- The brand voice note's register for each language.

## Safety

- No internal ids, hostnames or staff names.
- No promise of a date, a refund, a credit or a feature.
- No client pilot or NDA names.
- Security topics: acknowledge and route privately, never troubleshoot in public.

## Languages

- Translated for every interface language the company supports (from the knowledge base).
- Plan and product names left untranslated unless the brand note says otherwise.

## Versioning

- Owner, date verified, linked known issue.
- Re-verified monthly and on every related fix.
- Retired macros are archived with the reason, not silently deleted.

## Sign-off log

```text
| Macro | Reviewer | Date | Result | Fixes needed |
```
