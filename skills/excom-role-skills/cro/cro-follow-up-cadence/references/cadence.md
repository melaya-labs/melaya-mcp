# Default follow-up cadence

Use the team's cadence note when it exists. Otherwise use this default and propose it.

## By family

| Family | First follow-up | Second | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creator or affiliate invitation | Bump after 2 to 3 days | Closing note about 2 weeks later | Stop |
| Cold listing or integration invitation | Bump after about 7 days | Closing note about 7 days later | Stop |
| Sales outreach | "One more thing" with a smaller, new offer after about 10 days | none | Stop |
| Activation help to a user | "One more point" after about 10 days | none | Stop |
| Partner proposal after a call | Nudge after about 10 days | Nudge with news after 2 to 4 weeks | Continue while they engage |
| Agreement or MoU waiting | Nudge after about 10 days | Launch-day replies within the hour | |
| Warm thread, they replied | Same-day answer | | |

## Wording rules

- Same thread, never a new subject.
- Shorter than the mail before, one idea, the idea in one line ("Short version: ...").
- Ask for a one-word answer.
- Closing note: "I'll leave it here. If it becomes a priority, just reply to this thread."
- End cold mails with the opt-out line ("Not for you? Reply no and I won't write again.").

## Suppression list

- A human on the team already answered.
- "No", unsubscribe, or an out-of-office saying the person left.
- Hard bounce.
- An outreach pipeline already sent its own bump.
- Quiet hours, or the day's external message cap reached.
- An open decision with the owner on this deal.
- The contact is in a client pilot or under NDA.
