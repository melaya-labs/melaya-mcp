# Call brief template

```text
Call: <company, date, time with timezone, booking source>
Objective: <one sentence>
Good outcome: <plan chosen | template running | pilot scoped | partner joined | agreement next step>
Attendees: <name, role, decision power> | Our lead: <role>
Agenda (30 min): 5 discovery, 15 live demo, 5 objections, 5 next step
Product to show: <one, named as in the products note>
Workflows for their business: 1) 2) 3)
Proof: <validated template, public use case, partner page, integrator page>
Discovery questions: <five, below>
Objections and approved answers: <table>
Do not say: <claims and dates from the claims policy>
Next step to ask for: <one, dated>
Follow-up draft: <draft id>
```

## Discovery questions (pick five)

- What job do you want done every week, and who does it today?
- How often would it run, and on what systems?
- In the cloud or on your own machines?
- How many people would use it, and who approves actions?
- What would make this a success in 30 days?
- What have you tried before, and why did it stop?
- Who else decides, and how do you buy tools like this?

## Partner and integrator calls

- What do your clients ask you for that you cannot deliver today?
- How do you sell: referral, implementation, resale?
- Which two or three client use cases would you start with?
- What do you need from us: deck, templates, enablement, a joint post?

## Rules

- One page, every fact with a source (note, thread date, page fetched this turn).
- One product leads the demo.
- No personal data beyond name, role and company.
- Forbidden claims and unreleased dates listed explicitly.
