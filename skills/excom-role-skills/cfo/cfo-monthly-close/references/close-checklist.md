# Close checklist

## Pre-close (last two working days)

- Confirm which system is the company's books, and which apps feed it.
- List renewals and trials ending this month (vendor inventory, renewal mail).
- Remind contributors: the revenue role for affiliate disputes, the COO for vendor invoices received by mail.
- Run a preliminary payout-to-bank check on everything except the last day.

## Level 1: no dependencies (close day 1)

- [ ] Billing revenue summary for the month, payouts, refunds, credit notes, disputes
- [ ] Affiliate commissions created in the month
- [ ] Vendor and provider invoices collected from mail and consoles
- [ ] Payroll and contractor payments for the month (from the owner or payroll system)

## Level 2: needs level 1 (close day 2)

- [ ] Billing payouts reconciled to bank deposits
- [ ] Commissions reconciled to paid invoices (commission check skill)
- [ ] Deferred revenue: yearly plans recognised monthly
- [ ] Accruals for services received but not invoiced

## Level 3: needs level 2 (close day 3)

- [ ] Usage ledger sample: plan in billing against plan in the platform, mismatches listed for the admin
- [ ] Balance sheet reconciliations, plus the accounting system's close checklist or trial balance
- [ ] Adjusting entries proposed (a human posts them)

## Level 4: needs level 3 (close days 4 to 5)

- [ ] Draft P&L, balance sheet, cash flow
- [ ] Flux analysis with drivers
- [ ] Close brief and finance section of the monthly report (inserted after approval)
- [ ] Soft close, then hard close once the owner has reviewed
- [ ] Retrospective: one thing that slowed the close, one fix

## Default thresholds (when the team has none)

| Comparison | Investigate when |
|---|---|
| Actual vs budget | over 10 percent |
| Month over month | over 20 percent |
| Actual vs forecast | over 5 percent |

Write each driver as volume (more units, seats, runs, clicks) or rate (price, model, bid), plus whether it repeats.

## Reconciling item categories

- Timing: normal processing lag. Clears without action in 1 to 5 working days.
- Adjustment: needs an entry or a correction. A human posts it.
- Investigate: cause unknown. Document it and escalate if unresolved.

## Aging

31 to 60 days: follow up. 61 to 90 days: escalate to the owner. Over 90 days: propose a correction or write-off.

## Time zones

Billing, ad and accounting systems often count days in different time zones. Align them before comparing daily figures, and state which zone the brief uses.
