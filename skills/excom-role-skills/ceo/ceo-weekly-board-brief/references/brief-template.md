# Weekly brief template

Name the draft "Weekly brief YYYY-MM-DD" (the date of the Monday, or the team's brief day).

```text
Outcomes at a glance (read <time>)
  <outcome 1>: today / target, status green|amber|red, source tool and window
  <outcome 2>: ...
Decision 1: <one-sentence question the owner answers yes/no or by picking an option>
  Options: A / B / do nothing
  Recommendation: <option> because <reason>
  Owner: <role>   Due: <date>
  Evidence: <fact> (VERIFIED, tool, window) | <fact> (ASSUMED)
  What would change it: <signal>
Decision 2: same fields
Decision 3: same fields
Carried over: <last week's decision> -> taken / open / dropped
Red items: operations, cash, disputes, security (one line each, or "no change")
Not measured: <metric> because <app missing or no definition>
```

## Field rules

- Exactly three decisions. If fewer than three exist, write "no third decision this week" rather than padding the list.
- Every number carries its tool, its window and its read time. Never reuse a number from last week's brief.
- Status thresholds come from the team's goal tracker. If none are set, use these defaults: green at or above 90 percent of the straight-line expected value, amber at 70 to 90 percent, red below 70 percent.
- "No change" sections are one line.
- Leave out internal ids, customer names and anything a customer could be identified by.

## Independent views request (handoff text)

"For this week's brief: on <topic>, give at most five points, each tagged VERIFIED (tool, window) or ASSUMED, your recommendation, and what would change your mind. Answer before <time>."
