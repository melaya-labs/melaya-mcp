# Vault structure

## Folders

| Folder | Holds | First notes to write |
|---|---|---|
| 00 Home (a note at the root) | Entry point, key facts table, links to every folder hub | Home |
| Company | Positioning, brand voice, glossary, environment, competition | Positioning, Glossary, Brand Voice |
| Products | One note per product or plan, what it does, who it is for, limits | Products Hub, one note per product |
| Customers | Segments, ideal customer, personas, customer voice (no personal data) | Customer Segments, Customer Voice |
| Sales | Pipeline stages, offers, pricing and plans, partner and affiliate programme terms | Pricing and Plans, Affiliate Programme Terms, Partner Programme |
| Marketing | Channels and audiences, campaigns, content rules, tracking conventions | Channels and Audiences |
| Operations | Systems and connectors, vendors, runbooks, service levels | Systems and Tools, Vendor Inventory, one note per runbook |
| Finance | Metrics definitions, budget lines, close calendar, approval thresholds | Metrics Definitions, Budget and Thresholds |
| People | Team and owners by role, rhythms, policies (no salaries by name, no health data) | Team and Rhythms |
| Decisions | One record per decision | Decision Log Hub, one note per decision |
| _Templates | Note, decision, runbook and brief templates | Note Template, Decision Template |

Large teams can add one folder per executive role (for example "CFO") later, holding that role's playbooks and decision rules. Scope each executive to the company folders plus its own role folder.

## Home key facts table

| Fact | Value | Source | Date | Canonical note |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Keep 10 to 20 rows: what you sell, to whom, the current outcomes and targets, plans and prices (as a link), team size, main channels, and the systems of record (billing, CRM, analytics, accounting, affiliate platform, document storage).

## Notes executives look for by name

Name these clearly, because executives search for them: "metrics definitions", "pricing and plans", "affiliate programme terms", "partner agreements folder" (with the Drive folder id), "weekly rhythm", "reports folder", "approval thresholds", "runbooks", "vendor inventory", "risks and open questions".

## Decision record

Context, options considered, decision, owner, date, review date, reopen trigger, superseded by.
