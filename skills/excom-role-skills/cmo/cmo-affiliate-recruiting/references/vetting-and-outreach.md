# Vetting and outreach

## Prospect types

Editorial publishers and newsletters, consultants and agencies, complementary products, creators (video, podcast, social), community owners. They behave differently: publishers want an angle, consultants want a reason their clients benefit, creators want a story that fits their format.

## Audience-fit vetting

- Fit, not size: does their audience match the buyer of one specific product in the products note? A small audience of exact buyers beats a large general one.
- Engagement against followers or subscribers; sudden follower spikes, generic comments or engagement far out of line with reach suggest bought audiences.
- Brand safety: scroll the last three months for controversy, competitor deals or content that would attach badly to you.
- Authentic interest: have they covered your category unprompted?
- Promotion history: do their sponsored or affiliate posts still get engagement?
- Exclusions in the programme terms (self-referral, existing customers, own company accounts) checked before outreach.

Record one specific episode, video, issue or post per prospect; it is the reason for writing.

## Recruiting funnel with denominators

qualified, contacted, joined, first placement, first referred signup, first paid customer, retained productive affiliate. Count distinct people; several placements can belong to one affiliate. Joining is not activation.

## Outreach structure

- One message per prospect, in their language when you know it, register per the brand voice note.
- Subject names the one piece of their content; the first line says why that piece is the reason for writing.
- The offer in one sentence, exactly as the published terms state it, with the self-serve join link. No deck, no call unless they ask.
- One follow-up after a few days at most, never after a reply.
- No invented earnings examples, user counts or results.
- Anyone asking for terms outside the published programme goes to the owner; commercial questions go to the CRO.

## Disclosure

Every affiliate must disclose the material connection clearly near the link or in the video ("affiliate link", "#ad"), using the platform's paid-partnership label where one exists as well. Put the requirement in the activation kit and check the first placement. Regulated or cross-border campaigns go to the CLO.
