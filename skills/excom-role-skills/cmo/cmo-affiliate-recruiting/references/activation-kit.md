# Activation kit and monthly review

## What each new affiliate receives (within a week of joining)

1. A tested tracking link: open it, confirm attribution works end to end with authorised test data before a broad cohort is invited.
2. The brand kit location and approved creatives (`affonso_list_creatives` if the platform is Affonso).
3. One or two landing pages matched to their audience (`affonso_list_promotion_urls`), or a proposal for a new one.
4. Two or three true talking points about one product, from the products note. Never a script; they know their audience.
5. The disclosure requirement in plain words.
6. A proposed first placement: what, where, when (a newsletter section, a video segment, a resource page entry).
7. A support contact for questions.

## Terms decision table (for the CRO and owner, never invented)

| Decision | What the terms note must specify |
|---|---|
| Attribution | window, link versus code priority, repeat purchases |
| Eligible conversion | new versus existing customers, refunds and cancellations |
| Channel policy | brand bidding, coupon sites, paid ads, self-referral |
| Commission | base, rate, duration, tier changes |
| Payout | hold period, threshold, schedule |
| Changes and exit | notice, treatment of accrued commission |

If the note does not answer one of these, raise it with the owner rather than answering a prospect.

## Monthly review

- Actives: affiliates with at least one referred signup in the window (or the team's definition), counted from referral data, with the definition stated.
- Accounts without a referral: one useful nudge each (a new asset, a better landing page, a placement idea), never more than one per month.
- Top tenth: attention first; ask what would help them place more.
- Report referred signups, paid customers, refunds, and commissions pending, approved and paid, keeping new customers separate from upgrades.
- Attribution gaps (a referred signup that reached checkout without the referral attached) go to the CRO and engineering with the evidence.
- Cookie and code attribution assign credit; they do not prove incremental growth. Label comparisons honestly.
