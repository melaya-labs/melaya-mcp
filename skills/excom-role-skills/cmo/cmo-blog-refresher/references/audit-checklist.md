# Refresh audit checklist

## Current state

Basic: word count, last updated date, current position for the main query, monthly clicks.
Structure: number of H2 and H3, lists, tables, images, media, internal and external links.
Quality: readability, depth (shallow, medium, deep), unique insights, CTAs present.
Facts: every number, price, product name, competitor name and count, each marked keep, update or cut with its source.

Initial assessment:

```text
Title | URL | Primary query
Strengths: what works, sections to keep
Weaknesses: outdated facts, missing topics, weak sections
Quick wins: stats to refresh, broken links, missing FAQ
```

## Competitor audit (top 5)

For each: URL and position, word count, H2 and H3 outline, unique elements (data, examples, tools, visuals), last modified date. Prefer direct competitors, positions 1 to 3, recently updated pages.

## Refresh strategy

Immediate: update statistics, fix broken links, add high-priority topics, format the snippet answer, refresh examples and screenshots.
Structure: reorder H2s, FAQ from People Also Ask, comparison tables, a better opening.
Optimisation: internal links to add, authoritative sources to cite, image alt text, structured data.

## Writing rules

- Keep sections that rank, the voice, the URL, and anchors other pages link to.
- Visible "Last updated" line; the year next to every statistic.
- Paragraphs of 2 or 3 sentences, more H3s, lists and tables where they help, jump links on long pages.

## SEO checklist

- Title 50 to 60 characters, description 150 to 160.
- Primary query in the first 100 words; answer in the first 40.
- FAQ answers People Also Ask.
- Internal links to and from related pages.
- No promise of ranking or traffic gains in the plan.
