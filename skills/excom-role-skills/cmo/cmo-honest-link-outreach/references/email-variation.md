# Outreach targeting and email variation

## Targeting gate

| Signal | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ranking post or guide on an adjacent task | SEND | The search engine already trusts the URL for the topic. |
| Integration partner with a docs or integrations page | SEND | The relationship exists; the ask is a small favour. |
| Site with only login, pricing or careers pages indexed | CUT | No editorial page a link could sit on. |
| Topic stretch (your tool forced into an unrelated post) | CUT | Editors spot forced anchors; spam reports hurt every later send. |

## Routing by relationship

- Partner or customer: open with the real usage or integration in plain words; ask for a mention in their specific guide or doc; offer a case study or co-written content after written approval.
- Cold editor: open with what the product does in one plain sentence; ask for the exact section or list slot; offer a pasteable blurb, a screenshot or accurate data.
- Never a direct link swap, a fee, a free paid plan or any other value in exchange for the link.

Contact role: a marketing, content or SEO lead can make the edit; an engineer or founder gets a short "who owns the docs?" line.

## Ask first

State the exact link request in the first half of the email, then the offer. A request hidden behind praise or a "feature" offer reads as bait and gets ignored.

## Seven variation axes

In a batch of 5 to 10 emails, every pair differs on at least three axes, and adjacent emails never share subject style or opening.

| Axis | Options |
|---|---|
| Subject | lowercase page slug, brand pair, short question, section callout |
| Opening | their ranking URL, shared usage or integration, a point from their article, one direct question |
| Connection | data or API overlap, shared stack, a gap in their list, their own write-up |
| Ask | "could you add a one-sentence note", "would you be open to adding X under Y", "could we link the existing mention", "may I send a two-sentence blurb" |
| Offer | pasteable blurb, screenshot, co-written guide, case study (partners only) |
| Length | 3 sentences, 4 in two paragraphs, 5 plus blurb, 6 short with a handoff line |
| Sign-off | name only, "Best", "Thanks either way", "Cheers" plus a one-line PS |

## Pre-flight checklist

- Every SEND targets a specific ranking page and section.
- The request comes before the offer, and no offer is a payment, swap or free plan.
- Three or more axes differ between any two emails.
- 3 to 6 sentences, readable on a phone without scrolling.
- No em dashes, no buzzwords, no "I hope this finds you well".
- Every address verified.
