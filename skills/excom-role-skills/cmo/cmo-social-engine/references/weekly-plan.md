# Weekly plan

## Campaign spine (write once, adapt per platform)

1. Hook: one line that earns the next line.
2. Problem: the real friction the audience recognises.
3. Change: what is now possible.
4. Proof or boundary: a concrete mechanism, a visible control or an honest limitation.
5. Action: one destination and one reason to open it now, named rather than linked where the link policy forbids links.

## Default pillars (propose to the owner if the team has none)

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Product proof | 30% | a real run, a visible approval step, before and after |
| Education | 25% | how to do a task your buyers do, with or without you |
| Behind the build | 20% | decisions and trade-offs (feeds cmo-build-in-public) |
| Comparisons and honest trade-offs | 10% | where you fit and where a rival fits better |
| Community and partners | 10% | approved partners, user work shared with permission |
| Offers | 5% | programme or plan news, only as published |

Leave gaps for scheduled pipelines and real-time replies. Keep one to two weeks queued at most so posts stay relevant.

## Writing rules

- One central promise and one product per post.
- Adapt, never paste the same text across platforms.
- Hooks are a starting shape; fill them with a specific fact, number or story.
- No broetry, engagement bait, emoji bullets, manufactured vulnerability or em dashes.
- Short-form video: visual, spoken and on-screen text hooks in the first second, captions always on.

## Weekly review questions

- Which three posts did best and why (hook, format, topic, timing)?
- Which three did worst and what do we stop?
- Which posts brought first-party signups or affiliate and partner enquiries?
- What did the comments ask that a page or post should answer?
