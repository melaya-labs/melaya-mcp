# Prompt set, rates and foundations

## Building the prompt set

- About 20 prompts, written the way a buyer asks an assistant, not as keywords.
- Two or three per product or use case the team sells, taken from the products note.
- Three to five "[competitor] alternative" or "[you] vs [competitor]" prompts for the rivals that matter most.
- Two or three persona prompts ("best [category] for [persona]").
- Store the set with the date created; change it only deliberately and note each change, or month-to-month rates stop being comparable.

## Rates

- Cited rate: runs where any page of yours is a cited source, divided by runs. Always report n.
- Recommended rate: runs where your product is named as an option to choose, divided by runs.
- Rival rate: the same two rates for each tracked competitor.
- Framing note: how you are described when named (accurate, outdated, wrong). Wrong descriptions are a priority fix.

Three runs per prompt per month is the minimum; report "2 of 3", never "cited" from one run.

## Foundations checklist

- robots.txt: answer-engine and AI crawlers are allowed or blocked as the team decided (check the decision in the SEO note); no accidental block on public pages.
- llms.txt: present if the team uses one, and its counts and product list match the products note today.
- Rendering: key pages return full content without running scripts (fetch and check the text is there), exactly one canonical, structured data present.
- Datasets and numbers: published benchmark or data pages carry structured data with a working source link.
- Freshness: key pages show a last updated date and dated facts.

## Extractability checklist (per page)

- The direct answer in the first 40 words.
- At least one H2 phrased as the buyer's question, answered in one or two sentences.
- Comparison items in identical self-contained blocks.
- Facts and numbers with dates and sources.
- No answer hidden behind tabs, accordions that need scripts, or images of text.

## Where the gaps go

| Gap | Skill or role |
|---|---|
| Missing page for a prompt | cmo-content-coverage |
| Rival owns a comparison | cmo-vs-alternatives |
| Independent list or thread omits you | cmo-honest-link-outreach, cmo-reddit-serp |
| Absent from directories the answers cite | cmo-directory-submissions |
| Crawl, rendering or llms.txt problem | CPO by handoff |
