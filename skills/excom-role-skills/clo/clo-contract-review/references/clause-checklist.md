# Clause checklist and grading

## Grades
- IDEAL / ACCEPTABLE: quote the words that satisfy the position. No change.
- BELOW: between acceptable and walk-away. Propose a change and name the approver.
- WALK_AWAY: a walk-away trigger is present. Name it; signing needs the approver regardless.
- MISSING: silent after a manual synonym search. Evidence is "silent". Propose the fallback language.

## Clauses to review (minimum)
| Clause | Review points | Common issues |
|---|---|---|
| Limitation of liability | cap amount and basis, mutual or not, carve-outs, consequential damages exclusion, per claim or aggregate | cap at a fraction of low fees; carve-outs that swallow the cap |
| Indemnities | scope (IP, data breach, breach of warranty), mutual or not, cap, defence control | indemnity for "any breach"; no control of defence |
| IP and licences | pre-existing IP retained, developed IP, licence scope, feedback clause, open source | broad assignment; perpetual feedback licence |
| Data protection | DPA, controller or processor, subprocessors, breach notice, transfers, deletion | no DPA; blanket subprocessor consent; slow breach notice |
| Confidentiality | scope, term, carve-outs, return or destruction | indefinite terms beyond trade secrets |
| Warranties and disclaimers | scope, AI output disclaimer, survival | warranties of results from AI output |
| Term, renewal, termination | initial term, auto-renewal and notice, termination for convenience and cause, cure period, wind-down | short notice windows on auto-renewal |
| Governing law and disputes | law, venue, arbitration rules, waivers | remote venue; one-sided arbitration |
| Assignment | consent, change of control | assignment to a competitor allowed |
| Payment and taxes | net terms, late fees, price escalation, taxes | uncapped price rises |
| Publicity and marks | logo use, announcements, approval of wording | use of your marks without approval |
| Exclusivity and non-solicit | scope, territory, duration | exclusivity without minimum commitments |
| Commission or revenue share | against the published programme terms | non-standard rates, upfront fees |

## Reading rules
- Read cross-referenced clauses in full before grading.
- If one clause bears on two positions, grade both.
- Apply jurisdiction notes from the playbook; they can change the analysis.
