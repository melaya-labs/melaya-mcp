# Legal page parity checklist

Compare every language against the source language, page by page.

## Structure
- Same sections, same numbering, same order.
- No section missing or merged.

## Numbers and terms
- Every number matches: days, hours, percentages, amounts, age limits, notice periods.
- Defined terms translated consistently across all legal pages.
- Product and feature names spelled the same way as in the product.

## Parties and processors
- Company legal name and address identical.
- Subprocessors and data locations identical to the subprocessor list.
- Contact routes (privacy, security disclosure, support) identical.

## Promises
- No language promises more (shorter deadlines, extra rights) or less than the source.
- Status words translated faithfully (live, target, gap); no "certified" or "approved" that the source does not say.

## Release checks
- Previous public version archived.
- Acceptance version bumped when terms, usage policy or privacy changed.
- Each live page read after deploy; render errors and missing strings noted.
- Change log entry: date, page, sections, languages, acceptance version, reason.

## Style
- House style per language from the brand voice note.
- No em or en dash characters if the house style bans them.
