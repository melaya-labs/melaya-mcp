# Claim patterns to flag

| Pattern | Example | Why it matters | Rewrite direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Autonomy oversold (AI-washing) | "fully autonomous", "runs your company for you" | consumer protection and AI transparency rules | say what is automated and where a human approves |
| Guaranteed results | "doubles your revenue", "zero errors" | unsubstantiated performance claim | cite measured results with sample and date, or remove |
| Regulated activity | "trades for you", "manages your money", "gives legal advice" | can pull the product into licensed territory | describe the self-directed tool and who decides |
| Certifications not held | "SOC 2 compliant", "ISO certified", "endorsed by <vendor>" | false claim, contract exposure | state the real status or remove |
| Partner tier or endorsement | "official partner" when the tier is lower | breaks partner terms | use the exact tier wording the partner approved |
| Invented social proof | testimonials, logos, user counts without a source | deceptive | real, consented quotes only; counts with source and date |
| Stale competitor facts | prices, features, limits older than the page | misleading comparison | re-read the competitor page; concede where they win |
| Retired offers | old referral codes or schemes shown as live | misleading offer | link to the current programme terms |
| Urgency without basis | "only 3 left", "price rises tomorrow" | deceptive pressure | remove unless true and dated |
| Misspelt names | partner or competitor names | trademark and trust | use the owner's own spelling |

## Evidence rules
- Every claim names a source and the date read.
- Competitor facts come from the competitor's own page, read in this review.
- Partner claims need written approval of the exact wording, cited by date.
- Testimonials need written consent and unedited words (typos excepted).
- Numbers state the period, sample and source.

## Severity guide
Severity: 1 cosmetic, 3 misleading to some readers, 5 false or regulatory. Likelihood: 1 unlikely to be noticed, 5 certain to be read and relied on.
