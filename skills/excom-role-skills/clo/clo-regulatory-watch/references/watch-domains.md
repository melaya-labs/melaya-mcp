# Watch domains (examples; keep the ones your posture covers)

## AI and consumer protection
- AI transparency and deployer duties (for example the EU AI Act transparency articles).
- Consumer protection on AI claims and dark patterns (for example FTC guidance).

## Privacy and data
- GDPR and UK GDPR: rights, transfers, breach notice, records of processing.
- Other regimes where you have users (for example CCPA/CPRA, PDPA, LGPD, PIPL).
- Cookie and tracking consent rules.

## Online platforms and content
- Notice-and-action duties for hosting services (for example the EU Digital Services Act).
- Copyright takedown regimes and designated agents (for example DMCA).

## Finance and regulated activities (only if relevant)
- Crypto-asset and investment perimeter rules (for example MiCA, local virtual-asset regulators, securities regulators).
- Payments, consumer credit, money transmission.

## Distribution and platform policies
- App stores and desktop stores: data safety labels, age ratings, review guidelines.
- Integration marketplaces: privacy, uninstall and data deletion duties.
- Platform API policies: OAuth limited-use rules, endorsed-pathway rules for agents on enterprise APIs.

## Sources
Official journals and regulator sites first, then the platform's own policy pages and changelogs, then reputable law-firm summaries labelled as secondary.

## Status words
met | partly met | operational gap | not applicable | counsel needed. Never "compliant" or "certified" without the artifact.
