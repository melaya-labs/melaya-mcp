# Fairness checks for a brief and job description

## Job test (every essential)
- Could someone without it do the job well after normal induction and training? If yes, move to desirable or cut.
- Name the task the requirement serves. No task, no requirement.
- Year counts ("7+ years") stand for a capability: write the capability.
- Degree or credential: keep only if a law, licence or regulator requires it; otherwise "or equivalent experience".

## Exclusion test (every essential left)
A requirement applied to everyone can still disadvantage a group. Check for:
- Age proxies: "digital native", "recent graduate", "young team", "energetic".
- Gender-coded words: "rockstar", "ninja", "dominant", "aggressive", "nurturing".
- Disability: "must stand all day", "must drive" without a real task; describe the task and offer adjustments.
- Language and origin: fluency demands beyond what the work needs; "native speaker".
- Caring duties: always-on availability or unpaid overtime as a criterion.
For each one kept, write the legitimate aim and why the requirement is proportionate, and mark it for the owner to confirm.

## Conditions, not criteria
Right to work, notice period, location rules, background checks required by law, equipment. Checked the same way for every candidate, never scored.

## Wording
- "You" language, short bullets, grade 8 to 10 reading level.
- No internal acronyms, no superlatives, no hype words from the brand voice ban list.
- Pay: an approved range, or a "Verify" placeholder where local law requires a range. Never "competitive" alone.
- Adjustments line: "We will make reasonable adjustments to the application and interview process, and to the job. Tell <contact role> what would help."
