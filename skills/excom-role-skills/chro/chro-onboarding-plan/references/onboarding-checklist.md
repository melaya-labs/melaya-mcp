# Onboarding checklist templates

## Pre-start
- Access request recorded with reasons per project, approved by someone other than the requester.
- Account created by a platform admin with zero permissions.
- Seat available on the plan; invite proposed at the narrowest role.
- Welcome mail drafted: start time, time zone, who to contact, two-factor within 24 hours, desktop app or runner install.
- Equipment and software seats requested (spend, owner approves).
- Agreement signed (contractor) or offer accepted (employee).

## Day 1
- Meet the owner: purpose of the role, outcomes, how decisions and approvals work.
- Tour of the EXCOM board: executives, tasks, the approval queue.
- Install the desktop app or runner and link the computer; confirm online.
- Run one pipeline from a template end to end.
- Read the company overview, positioning and brand voice notes.

## Week 1
- Policy acknowledgements signed.
- Shadow one customer, partner or affiliate interaction.
- First small task tied to a team outcome, done by day 5.
- End-of-week check-in with the owner.

## 30 / 60 / 90 goals
Each goal: observable result, data source, linked team outcome, review date.

## Evidence lines (the record an auditor reads)
Request date | approver | account created date | invite role and date | two-factor on date | runner online date | acknowledgements dates | checklist closed date | sent to CISO date.

## Offboarding mirror
Keep the same record shape for exit: decision date, memberships removed, credentials rotated, devices and tokens revoked, verification timestamps.
