# Tone preset: Feature cards

> **Use when:** feature teasers, changelogs, onboarding, B2B products that should look like a real product page.

```yaml
id: tone-feature-cards
kind: tone
energy: "clean, professional, feature-forward"
scenes_per_20s: "4-6"
scene_length_s: "3-4"
transitions: "clean slide or wipe"
transition_s: "0.35-0.45"
type: "title case, medium weight, clean and readable"
voice: "feature, then benefit; third person; present tense"
hook: "product name plus tagline, clean"
highlight: "feature name plus one supporting line, shown in the real UI"
outro: "call to action or availability line"
music: "upbeat clean bed at 0.30-0.35"
sfx: "a consistent light layer: one soft cue per feature, 0.65-0.75"
share_copy: "{Product} is live. {Feature 1}, {feature 2} and {feature 3}, in one place."
```

## Example lines (fictional brand)

- Hook: "Brightfold Planner. Plans that update themselves."
- Highlight: "Smart Dependencies. Move one task, the rest follow."
- Outro: "Available now. Free for teams of five."

Do show each feature working in recreated UI. Don't fall back to stat cards or pill rows. Freeform direction from the user refines this preset; keep their words in the brief.
