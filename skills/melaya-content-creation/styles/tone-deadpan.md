# Tone preset: Deadpan

> **Use when:** products whose strongest asset is one quote or one quiet observation; when restraint makes it land harder.

```yaml
id: tone-deadpan
kind: tone
energy: "calm, dry; nothing registers as unusual"
scenes_per_20s: "3-4"
scene_length_s: "4-7"
transitions: "very slow crossfade, or a long hold"
transition_s: "0.8-1.0"
type: "mixed case, large, sparse, lots of empty space, one thought at a time"
voice: "minimal: one observation, then the product"
hook: "a quiet observation with no setup"
highlight: "one sentence per scene, no excitement"
outro: "the product name, maybe a very small tagline, a long hold"
music: "very quiet bed at 0.12-0.18"
sfx: "very sparse: 1-2 dry cues"
share_copy: "I made {product}. It {does the thing}."
```

## Example lines (fictional brand)

- Hook: "I used to water my plants."
- Highlight: "Now they text me."
- Outro: "Leafline."

Do let silence and space carry it. Don't add camera tricks or busy backgrounds. Freeform direction from the user refines this preset; keep their words in the brief.
