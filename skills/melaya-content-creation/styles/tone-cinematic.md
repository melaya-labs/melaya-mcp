# Tone preset: Cinematic

> **Use when:** launch films, products with natural scale (nature, space, infrastructure), grand claims.

```yaml
id: tone-cinematic
kind: tone
energy: "dramatic, trailer-scale; the product treated like a blockbuster"
scenes_per_20s: "4-5"
scene_length_s: "3-5"
transitions: "dramatic wipe, crossfade with scale (0.95 to 1.0), cinematic zoom"
transition_s: "0.5-0.8"
type: "ALL CAPS or heavy mixed case, full-bleed, very large"
voice: "epic, short declarative sentences, each landing before the next"
hook: "a sweeping statement about the world, said seriously"
highlight: "capabilities stated like superpowers"
outro: "the name full-screen, the tagline, the music swells"
music: "driving bed 118-130 BPM at 0.30-0.40, hardest cut on the drop"
sfx: "2-3 big cues plus interaction clicks"
share_copy: "{Product}. {Tagline, verbatim or lightly adapted}."
```

## Example lines (fictional brand)

- Hook: "FOR TOO LONG, MAPS STOPPED AT THE COASTLINE."
- Highlight: "Sonar depth. Live currents. Every reef."
- Outro: "DEEPCHART. The map goes on."

Do give each line its own beat and a strong downbeat. Don't use calm music under trailer motion. Freeform direction from the user refines this preset; keep their words in the brief.
