# Tone preset: Startup parody

> **Use when:** absurd or recursive concepts that land harder when played completely straight.

```yaml
id: tone-startup-parody
kind: tone
energy: "deadpan launch keynote energy; the joke is the seriousness"
scenes_per_20s: "4-5"
scene_length_s: "one claim per scene"
transitions: "hard cut, minimal crossfade"
transition_s: "0.0-0.2"
type: "sentence case, medium or heavy weight, monospace for data points, no decoration"
voice: "serious, matter-of-fact, never winking"
hook: "the problem, stated completely seriously"
highlight: "a feature or metric stated as fact"
outro: "product name, tagline, a URL that implies legitimacy"
music: "corporate bed at 0.25-0.35"
sfx: "sparse: one dry reveal hit, one UI accent, one logo payoff"
share_copy: "We built {product} to solve {problem, stated seriously}. {deadpan fact}."
```

## Example lines (fictional brand)

- Hook: "Every day, umbrellas protect us. But who protects the umbrellas?"
- Highlight: "Available in 12 rainy cities."
- Outro: "Shelterbrella. Rain cover for rain cover."

Do keep a straight face from the first frame to the last. Don't use winks, emoji or exclamation marks. Freeform direction from the user refines this preset; keep their words in the brief.
