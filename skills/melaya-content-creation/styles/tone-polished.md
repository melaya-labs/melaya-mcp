## Example lines (fictional brand)

- Hook: the product name alone, centred, held.
- Highlight: "Every change, reviewed before it ships."
- Outro: "Kettlebird. Notes that answer back."

Do hold longer and cut less. Don't add a second accent colour or any bounce. Freeform direction from the user refines this preset; keep their words in the brief.
