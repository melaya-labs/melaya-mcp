# Tone preset: Chaotic

> **Use when:** hype reels, memes, single-number launches, logistics parodies, anything with urgency.

```yaml
id: tone-chaotic
kind: tone
energy: "fast, loud, unhinged; the video is the joke"
scenes_per_20s: "6-8"
scene_length_s: "under 2 to 4"
transitions: "hard cut, flash cut, zoom cut (scale 1.2 to 1.0 on entry)"
transition_s: "0.0-0.25"
type: "ALL CAPS, heavy weight, slightly oversized, some words tilted"
voice: "short words, numbers, confidence over exclamation marks"
hook: "something that should not exist, at full volume"
highlight: "rapid fire: one word or one number per beat"
outro: "the name slams in, the tagline hits, cut to black"
music: "dense track at 0.35-0.45"
sfx: "dense: on most beats, at most two stacked"
share_copy: "{SHORT CLAIM}. {Product} is {overstated description}. Link below."
```

## Example lines (fictional brand)

- Hook: "GROCERIES WERE TOO SLOW."
- Highlight: "9 MINUTES. 40 AISLES. 0 CARTS."
- Outro: "DASHPANTRY. Dinner, now."

Do keep every read above the floor even at speed. Don't strobe, and don't flash text under 0.8 s. Freeform direction from the user refines this preset; keep their words in the brief.
