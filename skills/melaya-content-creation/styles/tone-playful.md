# Tone preset: Playful (default)

> **Use when:** most consumer and developer products with personality; when nothing else clearly fits.

```yaml
id: tone-playful
kind: tone
energy: "playful, clean, postable"
scenes_per_20s: "4-5"
scene_length_s: "3-5"
transitions: "crossfade, clean slide"
transition_s: "0.4-0.6"
type: "mixed case, comfortable weights, room to breathe"
voice: "first person plural, warm, direct, no corporate words"
hook: "a simple observation or question that sets up the reveal"
highlight: "short phrases, one idea per scene"
outro: "product name, then a tagline with a light punchline"
music: "upbeat bed at 0.30-0.40"
sfx: "moderate: 3-5 cues on reveals and taps"
share_copy: "Made {product}. It {does the thing, in its own words}. {best line}."
```

## Example lines (fictional brand)

- Hook: "Spreadsheets were built for numbers. Not for chasing people."
- Highlight: "Reminders write themselves."
- Outro: "Mossgrove. Invoices that chase themselves."

Do let the product be funny on its own terms. Don't force jokes, stack effects or rush reads. Freeform direction from the user refines this preset; keep their words in the brief.
