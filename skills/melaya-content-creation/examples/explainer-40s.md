# Worked example: 40 s narrated feature explainer (fictional brand "Kettlebird")

Kettlebird is a fictional AI workspace; the feature is a notes-folder integration: connect a local notes folder, choose what stays private, ask questions and get answers that cite the exact heading. All names and numbers are invented.

## Brief (short)

- **Deliverables:** 40 s (1200 frames) 1920x1080 for YouTube and LinkedIn, burned captions plus SRT and VTT, a 1280x720 thumbnail, share copy.
- **Tone / look:** tone-polished / look-light-ui-clean. Narrated (local TTS, speed 1.05), music bed ducked under the voice.
- **Rubric highlights:** strongest line "Your notes, finally useful."; flow: connect a folder, mark it local only, ask, get a cited answer, approve an edit.

## Script (narration about 2.9 words per second; reads headline-only, in narration order)

| Seg | Global (s) | Voiceover | On-screen read |
|---|---|---|---|
| s1 Hook | 0-7 | "Your notes know everything. Your AI knows none of it." | "Knows none of it." |
| s2 Connect | 7-15 | "Connect a folder. It's read in place: notes, links, tags." | "Notes. Links. Tags." (each word on its spoken word) |
| s3 Trust | 15-25 | "You decide what leaves your machine." | "Local only." |
| s4 Use and proof | 25-35 | "Ask anything. Kettlebird cites the exact heading, and proposes edits you approve." | "Cited. Approved." |
| s5 CTA | 35-40 | "Kettlebird. Your notes, finally useful." | the lockup (not burned again as a caption) |

## Storyboard

| Seg | Ground, camera | True morph | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| s1 | pale lavender; lens punch, then a fly-through of a note graph | the graph collapses into one blank page with a caret, on the last downbeat | voice from +0.35 s |
| s2 | ice blue; vertical push down a tall world | an indeterminate progress bar becomes a "connected" check | |
| s3 | pale mint; orbit around a settings panel, macro push on each switch | a toggle knob lifts out and becomes the lock icon of a "local only" chip | music breakdown spliced here (bar-aligned) |
| s4 | warm cream; orthographic schematic, lines draw themselves | a heading becomes a citation chip, then an approval card flips into the new task line | music lift lands on the product name |
| s5 | neutral light; radial contraction, then stillness | a folder outline morphs (96 points) into the mark, real-logo hand-off, glide into the lockup | ends at voice end + 2.4 s |

Joins between segments: zoom-flash, clock, blinds, card-fall (four different kinds). Captions: phrase captions at 44 px bold, bottom band reserved from the brief (nothing essential below y 950).

## Mix

Music at 0.34 ducked by the voice (ratio 12, release 650 ms), voice at 1.8, SFX bus 0.9 (about 25 soft cues), normalised to -14 LUFS and -1.5 dBTP, padded to 1200 frames. Light UI encodes at CRF 17 so small text stays crisp.

## Delivery

`kettlebird_explainer_16x9_en.mp4` (burned), the captionless master, `captions.en.srt` and `.vtt`, a thumbnail built from the s4 citation card at thumbnail scale ("Notes that cite"), the publishing package with three chapters (0:00 Connect, 0:15 Privacy, 0:25 Ask), each at least 10 s.
