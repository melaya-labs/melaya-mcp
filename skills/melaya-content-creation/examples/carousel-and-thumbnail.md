# Worked example: carousel, thumbnail and gallery (fictional brand "Paperlantern")

Paperlantern is a fictional recipe app that turns a photo of the fridge into three dinners. Deliverables are stills only. All names and numbers are invented.

## Brief (short)

- **Deliverables:** an 8-slide carousel at 1080x1350 (Instagram and a LinkedIn PDF), a 1280x720 YouTube thumbnail for the launch video, a Product Hunt gallery (4 images about 1270x760) and a 240x240 thumbnail.
- **Tone / look:** tone-playful / look-warm-organic. Real app screens recreated from the repo; fictional fridge contents.

## Carousel

| Slide | Content | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | "Your fridge has 3 dinners in it." over a recreated camera screen pointed at a (fictional) fridge shelf | 6 |
| 2 Problem | "It's 7 pm. You have half an onion and no plan." | 11 |
| 3 Step 1 | "Snap the shelf." with the real capture screen | 3 + UI |
| 4 Step 2 | "Paperlantern spots what's there." with the real ingredient chips, one marked "use soon" | 5 + UI |
| 5 Step 3 | "Pick a dinner." three real recipe cards, times from the app's own data | 3 + UI |
| 6 Proof | the finished recipe screen with its step list | 0 + UI |
| 7 Summary | "Snap. Pick. Cook." | 3 |
| 8 CTA | logo, "paperlantern.example", "Free on iOS and Android" (an approved fact) | 6 |

Grid: 72 px margins, a progress mark "3/8" top right, a recipe card bleeding across the right edge of slides 3-5 to invite the swipe. Heading 88 px, body 40 px. Exported as PNG per slide and as one PDF.

## Thumbnail (launch video)

"3 dinners. 1 photo." in heavy type on the left half, the recipe card bleeding off the right edge, the logo small top left. Tested at 168 px wide on light and dark backgrounds: readable. It shows a moment that is in the video.

## Product Hunt gallery

1. The hook visual with the tagline (60 characters at most): "Snap your fridge, get three dinners".
2. The capture screen in a device frame (15 degree turn, contact shadow).
3. The ingredient chips with "use soon".
4. The three recipe cards.
Thumbnail 240x240: the logo on the warm ground. The maker's first comment: why it exists, who it is for, what is next, an honest request for feedback.

## Checks

Every screen is a recreation from source with fictional data; the only number claims are approved facts; every image is at its platform size; fonts and icons are in the asset ledger with their licences.
